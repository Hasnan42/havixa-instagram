Starbucks spends about $400M a year on software. Now it is using AI to build its own.

According to Bloomberg, Starbucks is building in house replacements for a Microsoft inventory system and an IBM maintenance platform, alongside its own point of sale to replace Oracle. The first tools could roll out by late 2027.

The savings so far are smaller than the headlines suggest (about $30M expected in 2026). But the signal is loud: when AI makes custom software cheap to build, paying forever for tools you barely use gets harder to justify.
